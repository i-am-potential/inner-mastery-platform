Key Practices
Practice 01Embodied Check-In
▾
  • Pause at any transition point — before a call, between tasks, before meals
  • Feel both feet on the floor
  • Take one slow exhale
  • Name 3 physical sensations you notice right now
"What am I noticing right now — in my body, not my head?"
Practice 02Single-Tasking Window
▾
  • Choose one routine activity per day (coffee, a walk, eating lunch)
  • Do it without a phone, screen, or background noise
  • When your mind drifts, gently return attention to the physical experience
  • No evaluation of how well you did — just return
"What is actually happening right now, not what I'm thinking about?"
Practice 0315-Minute Launch Rule
▾
  • When a task feels heavy or you notice resistance, set a 15-minute timer
  • Commit only to starting — stopping at 15 minutes is fully allowed
  • Before starting, say or write: "I'm doing this because I value ___."
  • Momentum is a bonus, not the goal
"Can I start for just 15 minutes?"
Practice 04Morning Values Priming
▾
  • Read your values statement within the first hour of your day
  • Choose one value to embody before noon
  • Name one specific action that will reflect that value today
"What kind of man am I becoming today?"
Weekly Reflection
  1. When did I feel most present this week — what was happening, and what made contact easier?
  2. When did I slip into auto-pilot — what triggered it, and how long before I noticed?
  3. Where did my values stay accessible under pressure, and where did they fall away?
  4. Which actions this week felt values-driven versus just reactive or obligatory?
  5. What's one condition I want to practice staying present in next week?
